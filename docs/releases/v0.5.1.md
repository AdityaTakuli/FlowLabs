FlowLabs: data, SQL and machine learning in one place. Free and open source (MIT).

## Changes

- **A calmer side menu.** Step names are smaller. Hovering one grows it smoothly, and the selected
  step stays larger and fully lit. The page's sections underneath behave the same way.

## Fixes

- **Dialogs fit small windows.** In a short window, the update dialog (and every other dialog) was
  cut off at the top. Dialogs now keep their title and buttons on screen, and their content
  scrolls.
- **Updates wait until they are ready.** GitHub publishes a new release a few minutes before its
  files are uploaded. FlowLabs no longer announces an update during those minutes. It waits until
  the file for your system is there, so **Install and restart** works straight away.

Everything else is as in [0.5.0](https://github.com/AdityaTakuli/FlowLabs/releases/tag/v0.5.0):

- the Analyze step with in-project SQL and Ask AI;
- the Charts step;
- charts that expand or open in their own window;
- the scroll-highlight side menu.

## Install or update

From 0.4.0 or later, click **Update to 0.5.1** in the top bar. Otherwise, download the file for
your system below.
