FlowLabs: data, SQL and machine learning in one place. Free and open source (MIT).

## Changes

- **Easier to read.** Small grey text, coloured labels and the Train button now meet the contrast
  that Apple's guidelines ask for (4.5:1), in both light and dark appearance.
- **A clearer side menu.** Every step is readable, not only the current one. The current step sits
  on a raised highlight, like a sidebar on a Mac, and the page's sections below it are marked with
  a line.
- **Match system appearance.** The appearance button now cycles Match system, Light and Dark, so
  you can always go back to following your computer's setting.
- **Calmer tab switches.** Changing steps fades quickly instead of sliding.
- **SQL scripts run like MySQL Workbench.** `DELIMITER`, trigger and procedure bodies work, and
  `USE` is allowed on read-only connections.
- **One-command installers** for Linux, macOS and Windows. They retry when the download drops.

## Fixes

- **Narrow windows and phones.** A wide table could stretch the whole window, which cut off pages
  and the bottom step bar. Pages now fit, and wide tables scroll on their own.
- **Pipeline buttons** (move a step up or down, remove) are named for screen readers.

## Install or update

From 0.4.0 or later, click **Update to 0.5.2** in the top bar. Otherwise, download the file for
your system below.
